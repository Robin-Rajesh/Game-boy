How to Play

Drag anywhere to steer Lio. Your finger never has to cover the ember.

Lio sings automatically, on the beat. Every shot is a note in the song.

Only the white core of your flame can be hit. Graze bullets to charge your Crescendo.

When the ♪ button glows, tap it (or two-finger tap, or press Space) to unleash a wave that clears every bullet.

Collect the 4 Echoes in each chapter. Each one brings a new instrument back into the music.

Golden sparks strengthen your flame. Friends you save join your Choir and sing with you.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows · Shift = precise · Space = Crescendo · P = pause
